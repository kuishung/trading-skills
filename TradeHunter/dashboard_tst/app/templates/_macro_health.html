How it works - nothing to do by hand. The web app fetches every series once a day (and 90 s after it starts) from FRED's public download - no API key - and from Yahoo for the dollar index, adding new observations and updating revised ones (GDP and the price indexes are revised after their first print). The series marked topic feed the Insights → Macro page; the rest feed the macro study board.
