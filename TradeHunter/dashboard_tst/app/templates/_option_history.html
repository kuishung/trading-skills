How it updates - nothing to do by hand. Massive publishes each trading day's options file about 11:00 New York time the next day. The screener collector on Hermes asks for it from 11:15 ET (every hour until it is out), files every stock's implied volatility from it, then converts it to Parquet within a minute. Massive re-publishes old days now and then: the collector checks weekly and converts those again. The Parquet files are the Options page's Contract history and the past dates of its Volatility surface. After the close the collector also saves Massive's closing IVs (part 4) - those days' surfaces use the closing quote instead of the last trade.
