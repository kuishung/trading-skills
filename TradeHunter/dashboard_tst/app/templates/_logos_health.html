How it works - nothing to do by hand. The web app downloads each ticker's logo once from a free public logo service ({{ lg.sources | join(" or ") }}, no key needed) and keeps it on this server ({{ lg.dir }}), so pages show it at once. Your members' watchlist, MATP and Curated tickers go first, then every stock and ETF in the options data; it looks again every {{ lg.warm_every_h }} hours, refreshes a logo after {{ lg.keep_days }} days, and asks again after {{ lg.miss_days }} days for a ticker no service had. Until a logo is stored, the page shows the ticker's letters.
